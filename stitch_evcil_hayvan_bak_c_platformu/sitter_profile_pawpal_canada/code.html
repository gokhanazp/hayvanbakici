About Emily Watson

Hi there, fellow pet parents! 🐕 I'm Emily, a lifelong dog enthusiast living in downtown Toronto near Queen Street West and Trinity Bellwoods. I have over 6 years of experience caring for pups of all sizes, from rambunctious golden retrievers to anxious rescue terriers.

I believe dog care should be calm, joyful, and completely transparent. That’s why every single walk includes a real-time GPS tracking route, photo check-ins, water bowl refresh, and a thorough paw wipe down before coming inside (crucial for Toronto winters and muddy park days!).

I work flexibly from home, so boarding dogs receive continuous human company, cozy couch snuggles, and scheduled romps in my safely double-gated backyard. I am trained in force-free positive reinforcement handling and comfortable administering daily oral medications.
