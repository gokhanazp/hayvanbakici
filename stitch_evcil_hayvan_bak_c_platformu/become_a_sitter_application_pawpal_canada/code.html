Frequently Asked Questions

Can I set my own rates? expand_more

Yes! You maintain full control over all your prices for walks, boarding, and visits. You can adjust your rates whenever you wish.

How & when do I get paid? expand_more

Earnings are released every Tuesday via Canadian Direct Deposit (EFT) or Interac e-Transfer straight to your preferred bank account.

What about taxes in Canada? expand_more

We provide an automated annual T4A summary every February to make filing your Canadian tax return with CRA straightforward.
