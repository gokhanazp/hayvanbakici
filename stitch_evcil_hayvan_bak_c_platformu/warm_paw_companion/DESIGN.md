## Brand & Style

This design system embodies warmth, gentle care, and joyful reassurance for modern pet parents and reliable pet sitters. It balances playful warmth with uncompromising reliability: pet care is deeply personal and emotionally charged, requiring a visual presence that feels both endearing and rigorously competent.

The aesthetic blends **Soft Playful Tactility** with **Warm Modern Minimalism**:
- **Personality**: Compassionate, sunny, protective, and cheerful without drifting into childishness or cluttered amateurism.
- **Target Audience**: Pet guardians seeking dependable sitters and walkers, as well as vetted sitters offering dedicated, loving service.
- **Emotional Response**: Immediate visual warmth, a sigh of relief, unconditional trust, and an encouraging sense of comfort.
- **Key Motifs**: Soft pebble silhouettes, pill tags, cushioned containers, gentle organic borders, and deliberate micro-interactions that convey kinetic bounce and responsiveness.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from sunlit parks, natural earth, and cozy domestic warmth. It avoids sterile cool grays and harsh blacks in favor of comforting culinary and natural tones.

- **Primary (`#3D7A68`)**: A balanced, comforting sage forest green that anchors trust, medical-grade safety, and outdoor vitality. Used for primary actions, verified badges, active navigation states, and grounded structural accents.
  - Light Tint / Surface: `#EBF3EF` (Sage Mist) for quiet highlight regions and badge fills.
- **Secondary (`#F37B5C`)**: A spirited, joyful warm coral/peach that evokes loving companionship, playfulness, and immediate engagement. Reserved for primary CTAs, booking indicators, urgent alerts, and celebratory micro-moments.
  - Light Tint / Surface: `#FDEEE9` (Peach Foam) for secondary pill badges and soft hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#F4B342`)**: A radiant honey gold representing joy, treats, star ratings, and energetic daylight walks.
  - Light Tint / Surface: `#FEF7EA` (Honey Glaze) for status alerts, highlights, and subtle banner backings.
- **Neutral (`#53483E`)**: A warm, espresso-tinted charcoal that delivers softer contrast than pure black, ensuring accessible text without cold mechanical rigidity.
  - Neutral Muted: `#84776D` for subheadings, captions, and secondary icons.
  - Neutral Border: `#EFE7DE` for delicate surface divisions.
- **Canvas / Surfaces**:
  - Base Canvas: `#FDFBF7` (Warm Vanilla Milk).
  - Elevated Container / Card: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Cloud White) to pop gently against the vanilla backdrop.
  - Inset Canvas: `#F7F2EB` (Warm Sand) for wells, search bars, and muted segment controls.

## Typography

The type system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels, taking advantage of its rounded geometric terminals, open apertures, and innate friendliness. It reads as conversational and welcoming without sacrificing optical clarity in data-dense schedules or booking flows.

- **Headlines & Display**: Set with a firm weight (`700` and `800`) and slightly tightened tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to produce cohesive, memorable word-shapes.
- **Body Text**: Tuned for relaxed legibility at `15px` and `17px` with generous proportional leading to accommodate comfortable scanning of pet profiles, medical requirements, and sitter bios.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Maintained at higher weights (`600` and `700`) with modest positive tracking to prevent ink-trapping and keep badges legible at reduced scales.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid, responsive 12-column grid on desktop/tablet and a 4-column structure on mobile devices, pinned against soft maximum containers (`1200px` for content sheets, `1440px` for wide dashboards).

- **Spatial Rhythm**: An 8pt spatial cadence sets baseline rhythm. Element gaps within card clusters use `space-sm` (`8px`) or `space-md` (`16px`), while full section transitions expand generously to `space-xl` (`40px`) or beyond, preserving uncluttered air around pet photography and profile highlights.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: Canvas margins drop to `16px` (`margin-mobile`), multi-column profile cards reflow into a clean vertical carousel or stack, and primary actions pin to a floating bottom navigation cushion.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `24px` margins; sitter search filters condense into a persistent horizontal pill shelf.
  - **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12-column layout with `40px` margins; two-column asymmetric split layouts (sticky map / booking summaries on the right, profile feeds on the left).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses warm ambient diffusion rather than stark drop shadows, avoiding cold corporate grays in favor of cocoa-tinted shadows that sit harmoniously on vanilla surfaces.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Transparent or resting directly on the `#FDFBF7` canvas, used for utility panels, background chips, and inline forms.
- **Level 1 (Card / Resting Tile)**: `#FFFFFF` background with an ambient warm shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(83, 72, 62, 0.05)`, accompanied by a microscopic border (`1px solid #EFE7DE`) to prevent blending against pale surfaces.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Floating Cards)**: Lifted state for interactive sitter profiles and clickable modules: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(83, 72, 62, 0.08)`, moving the container `-2px` along the Y-axis.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Sticky Bars / Flyouts)**: High prominence elements: `0 20px 36px -6px rgba(83, 72, 62, 0.12)`.
- **Inner Wells**: Inset surfaces (search bars, calendar date wells) employ `box-shadow: inset 0 2px 4px rgba(83, 72, 62, 0.04)` to convey a soft, touchable tactile depth.

## Shapes

The geometric approach embraces continuous, human-friendly curvature. Sharp right angles are strictly avoided to sustain a gentle, safe, and welcoming environment.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Form inputs, dropdown rows, and micro-controls.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`)**: Standard sitter cards, booking summary panels, and modal containers.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`)**: Hero featured cards, media wrappers, and playful banner frames.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Interactive buttons, status chips, notification count dots, and tag badges.
- **Squircle Avatar Frames**: Pet and caregiver profile pictures are framed either as full circles or organic smooth rounded squares (`border-radius: 28%`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Coral)**: Background `#F37B5C`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, full pill radius (`9999px`), padding `12px 24px`. On hover, translate `-2px` vertically with a playful micro-spring animation (`transition: transform 0.25s cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`) and drop shadow `0 6px 16px rgba(243, 123, 92, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Action (Sage Mist)**: Background `#EBF3EF`, text `#3D7A68`, font `label-lg`, border `1px solid #C8DDD4`. Hover scales slightly with a soft tint shift to `#DCECE4`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background, text `#53483E`, hover background `#F7F2EB` with a heart or paw icon accent.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Status Chips**: Height `28px`, padding `0 12px`, radius `9999px`, font `label-md`.
  - *Verified Sitter*: Surface `#EBF3EF`, text `#3D7A68`, accompanied by a small green check/shield icon.
  - *Instant Book*: Surface `#FEF7EA`, text `#B07817`, accompanied by a tiny lightning paw icon.
  - *New / Special*: Surface `#FDEEE9`, text `#F37B5C`.
- **Filter Chips**: Resting state has a `#FFFFFF` fill, `#EFE7DE` border, `#53483E` text. Active state adopts full `#3D7A68` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards
- **Sitter & Pet Cards**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with `16px` border-radius, `1px solid #EFE7DE` outline, and Level 1 elevation.
- Structure:
  - Top: High-aspect photo frame with rounded internal corners (`12px`), featuring overlaid pill tags (e.g., "5.0 ★ (42)").
  - Body: Pet or sitter name in `title-md`, subtle badge row for pet types accepted (Dog, Cat, Small Pet), location distance, and pricing displayed cleanly in `headline-sm` with a sage tone.
  - Hover: Elevation lifts to Level 2 with a gentle `0.2s` transition.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #EFE7DE`, radius `12px`, text `body-md` in `#53483E`, placeholder in `#84776D`. Focus state transitions the border to `#3D7A68` with a soft outer ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(61, 122, 104, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Size `22px`, radius `6px` for checkboxes and `9999px` for radios. Selected state fills with `#3D7A68` displaying a white checkmark or center dot. Check action incorporates a subtle bouncy pop scale (`transform: scale(1.1)` transitioning to `scale(1.0)`).

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Paw Progress Bar**: Step tracker for dog-walk tracking and booking steps, using playful rounded track bars and a paw icon indicator at the active node.
- **Pet Trait Pill**: Compact micro-chips displaying medical, temperament, or dietary notes (e.g., "Loves Tennis Balls", "Needs Insulin", "Cat Friendly") in soft pastel vanilla/cream tones.
